SAUDICO Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 30, 2026

Introduction

SAUDICO is an attendance management application designed to help manage and record trainee attendance.

Information We Collect

SAUDICO does not allow users to create accounts within the application. Users can only sign in using an account that has already been provided to them.

When users sign in, SAUDICO may process information required to authenticate their account, such as their email address or user identifier.

SAUDICO does not intentionally collect photos, audio recordings, precise location data, or physical address information.

How We Use Information

Information processed during authentication is used to:

  • Authenticate users.
  • Provide access to the application.
  • Identify the authenticated user.
  • Provide attendance-related functionality.

Third-Party Services

SAUDICO uses Firebase services provided by Google to support authentication and application functionality.

Firebase may process information necessary to provide these services.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the application and to prevent unauthorized access.

Data Sharing

SAUDICO does not sell users' personal information. Information is not shared with third parties except where necessary to provide services used by the application, such as authentication and backend services.

Data Retention

Authentication and account-related information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide access to the application and its services.

Children's Privacy

SAUDICO is intended for trainees and authorized users. The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to its functionality.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date above.

Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy or SAUDICO, please contact the application administrator.
